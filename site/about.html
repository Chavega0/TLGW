The academy

More than a sports center.

Stamina11 is a boutique youth sports academy in Dubai Studio City, built around one conviction: children do not need a bigger gym, they need better attention.

What boutique means here.

Small groups. A coach who knows your child's name, their fears, and the exact skill they are one week away from landing.

Olympic-level coaching. Our coaches have trained and competed at the highest levels of their sports. They teach beginners on purpose: foundations are where champions are actually made.

One community. Gymnasts share a corridor with swimmers, grapplers and dancers-in-disguise. Kids see other kids working hard, and it normalizes the thing every parent hopes for: effort.

What we believe.

01

Discipline is a gift

Not a punishment. A child who learns to practice owns a tool that outlasts every sport and every season.

02

Joy is the fuel

Nobody repeats what they hate. Sessions are engineered so the hard parts feel like the good parts.

03

Progress must be visible

Parents see milestones, sessions and announcements in the Stamina11 app. No guessing what happened behind the glass.

04

Every child is an athlete

Some will compete. All will carry strength, courage and body confidence into the rest of their lives. Both outcomes count as winning.
