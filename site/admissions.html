Admissions

Three steps to the floor.

No trials in front of a crowd, no season-long commitments before you have met a coach. Joining Stamina11 starts with one relaxed session.

01

Assess

Book a one-on-one assessment. A coach spends the session on the floor with your child, watching how they move, listen and light up. Parents watch from up close.

02

Place

Within a day you get a recommendation: the program, the level and the group that fits. You meet the coach who will actually run those sessions before you commit.

03

Progress

Training begins. Schedules, payments, announcements and milestones live in the Stamina11 app, so you always know what happened on the floor today.
