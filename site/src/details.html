Implementation

What a project can include.

Every project is scoped on the proposal, so the list below is what we draw from, not a fixed package. Smaller improvements to an existing setup draw from the same list.

Setting up

  • Pipeline stages, with written rules for when a deal moves forward
  • Lead routing: in turn, by territory, or to whoever is on duty
  • Phone system connected to the CRM, calls logged with recordings
  • Email sequences and the rules that stop them
  • Automations between tools, documented, and AI agents built on Claude or OpenAI for the routine steps
  • The revenue scoreboard: targets, leaderboards and the reports the owner actually reads

Moving in and handing over

  • Data migration from your current system or spreadsheets, duplicates merged
  • The old system read-only during the switch, then yours to close
  • Training sessions on your own data, recorded for the team
  • Handover notes: how each piece runs and who to call
  • A named admin seat you create for us and revoke at handover
  • Custom tools where no platform covers a step

Not usually in scope: Salesforce or ERP migrations, writing your email copy, or running your outbound for you. We build it; your team sends it. If the system you run jobs on is not on the list above, ask on the call.

Buying through Stacktik

How a purchase works.

  1. 01Shortlist with costs

    Two or three options that fit the workflow, each with its monthly cost for your seat count, on the proposal.

  2. 02Purchase through the program

    Where it helps, we set the plan, seats and phone numbers up through the vendor's program and apply any customer offer it provides. Where it does not help, we tell you to buy direct.

  3. 03Your agreement, your account

    The subscription is on the vendor's plan and pricing, and how it is billed is on the proposal. We work inside your account through a seat you control.

  4. 04Relationship disclosed

    The proposal states, next to every tool, whether the vendor pays us anything and through which program. You see the incentive before you see the price.

After the work

Ongoing support, if you want it.

Optional and monthly, for teams that want the people who built the system to keep it current. What it covers and what it costs go on the proposal like everything else.

Questions

Questions the home page doesn't answer.

Do we have to buy the software through you?

No. Buying through our programs is how vendor offers apply and how we hold one thread with the vendor on your behalf. You can buy direct and hire us only for the implementation, or buy through us and run the setup yourself.

What do you charge?

The monthly software cost for each tool is on the proposal, with any partner offer a program provides shown next to it, and so is whether help choosing and buying carries a fee on its own. Implementation and improvement work is quoted as a fixed fee, in writing, before it starts. Ongoing support is optional and monthly. Nothing is charged for the first call.

How do you work inside our accounts?

Through an admin seat you create for us and can revoke at handover. Your data stays in your accounts.
