Where people start

Five ways to start. One team.

You will probably recognise one of these. Each is a different starting point, and the same people handle all of them.

"Which CRM or phone system should we buy?"

We map how your team sells and supports today, then shortlist the tools that fit, with the monthly cost of each, so you decide on fit and cost, not on demos.

"Can you help us get this software?"

Yes. We handle the purchase through our partner programs where it helps: the right plan and seat count, any partner offer the program provides, and one contact for the whole purchase.

"We have the tools, but they don't work together."

We connect what you own: calls logged to the CRM, leads sent to the right rep, follow-ups going out on time, reports pulled from every system. Replace only what has to go.

"We need better outbound, or follow-up that doesn't rely on memory."

Call logging, AI call notes, email sequences and rep-to-rep handoffs, built into the tools you already use. A missed call becomes a callback task, a reply stops the sequence, and a booked call lands on the record.

"We bought it. We need it set up properly."

Setup, your data moved over, the tools connected to each other, and training, across every tool rather than one vendor's, scoped in writing. If the vendor's own onboarding is enough for your case, we say so.

From lead to revenue

What happens to a lead, step by step.

The same path for every lead, whether it arrives by form, phone, email or chat. Terracotta marks where an AI agent does the work. Everything else is a rule the tools follow on their own.

  1. 01

    A lead comes in

    Web form, phone call, email or site chat. Your team types nothing.

    FormPhoneChat
    AISite chat answers the first question and captures who they are and what they need.
  2. 02

    Captured in the CRM

    A record is created, duplicates merged, the source attached, missing details filled from your contact-data tools.

    CRMContact data
    RuleNo judgement needed here, so no AI. The tools do it the same way every time.
  3. 03

    Qualified

    Checked against your fit rules: size, location, what they are asking for, and whether they are already a customer.

    CRM
    AIAn agent built on Claude or OpenAI reads the message, scores the fit, and declines politely when it isn't one.
  4. 04

    Routed to a rep

    An owner within minutes, in turn or by territory. A task is created and the rep's phone rings.

    CRMPhone
    RuleRouting is a rule you set once. It never needs to guess.
  5. 05

    First contact

    The rep calls from inside the CRM or sends the first reply. The record is already open.

    PhoneEmail
    AIThe first reply is drafted in your tone with the details already in, for the rep to approve and send.
  6. 06

    Logged, automatically

    Recording and transcript land on the record. The next step is set.

    PhoneCall notes
    AICall notes and next steps are written to the CRM without anyone typing.
  7. 07

    Follow-up runs

    A sequence goes out until they reply. A reply stops it and books the call.

    OutreachAutomation
    AIFollow-ups drafted and timed from the conversation so far. The rep approves; the sequence stops itself on a reply.
  8. 08

    Won, and on the scoreboard

    The stage moves, revenue counts, the leaderboard updates for the whole team.

    CRMReporting
    AIStale deals flagged, next actions suggested, a weekly summary written for the owner.

Before you book

Common questions.

We already have software. Can you work with it?

Yes. The proposal says what to keep, what to connect and what, if anything, to replace, and we won't recommend a switch we can't justify in writing.

What happens if we buy the software through you?

We set the purchase up through the vendor's program: plan, seats, phone numbers, and any customer offer the program provides. The monthly cost and how the subscription is billed are on the proposal. You can also buy direct and hire us only for the implementation, or buy through us and run the setup yourself.

What do you charge?

The monthly software cost for each tool is on the proposal, with any partner offer a program provides shown next to it, and so is whether help choosing and buying carries a fee on its own. Implementation and improvement work is quoted as a fixed fee, in writing, before it starts. Ongoing support is optional and monthly. Nothing is charged for the first call.

Do your partnerships bias your advice?

They shape it, so we show them. We are not a neutral marketplace of everything; we know the platforms in our programs best, and the proposal states our relationship with each tool next to its price. If a tool outside our programs fits you better, that is what goes on the proposal.

Who does the work?

A technical RevOps consultant assigned specifically to your project, with you from the ideation stage to fully deployed: the same person on the call, the proposal and the build, and able to build anything the project needs, from the CRM setup to a custom tool. Stacktik is based in Woodbridge, Ontario, and the handover notes say who built each piece and who to call about it.

What if nothing off the shelf does what we need?

Then we build it: an internal tool, a customer portal, or a connector between two systems, scoped and quoted on the same proposal and living inside the same CRM, calling and reporting setup.

How do you use AI?

Where it does routine work reliably. AI call notes written to the record. Agents built on Claude and OpenAI that qualify inbound leads, draft follow-ups for a rep to approve, and keep the CRM current. Chat on your site that answers the first question and hands off the rest. Every agent runs inside your tools with its source attached, and a person approves anything that reaches a customer.

What do you mean by a revenue scoreboard?

Targets per rep and per team, a live leaderboard of calls, booked meetings and closed deals pulled from the CRM and the phone system, and the owner's revenue and pipeline view, all updating without anyone typing. Reps see the score. Owners see what is coming.

What happens on the call?

We ask what you use, what isn't working, and what you are trying to get to. You get a straight answer on whether the next step is software, implementation, both, or nothing yet. If there is a fit, a written proposal follows.
