The collection

Eight programs. One floor.

Walk the room. Every program is listed the same way: what it is, who it serves, and what your child takes away from it. When in doubt, start with an assessment and we will point you to the right door.

Artistic Rhythmic Toddler Gym Trampoline Swimming Reformer Pilates Kickboxing Jiu-Jitsu

Artistic Gymnastics

Bars, beam, vault and floor. The discipline every other sport borrows from: strength, flexibility, spatial awareness and the habit of trying again.

Format
Small groups by level, recreational to competitive track
Builds
Total-body strength, coordination, courage, discipline
Setting
Sprung floor, competition apparatus, dedicated coaches

Rhythmic Gymnastics

Ribbon, hoop, ball, clubs and rope. Strength disguised as grace, set to music. A sport of precision that rewards musicality as much as athleticism.

Format
Small groups by level, recreational to competitive track
Builds
Flexibility, balance, artistry, focus under pressure
Setting
Full-size carpet, apparatus provided, choreography included

Toddler Gym

First climbs, first rolls, first confidence. A soft, structured introduction to movement for the youngest athletes, with a grown-up close at hand.

Format
Parent-and-child and early independent classes
Builds
Motor skills, balance, listening, social confidence
Setting
Padded early-years zone, sized for small hands and feet

Trampoline

Air awareness is a superpower, and this is where it is trained. Controlled bouncing, shapes, landings and, in time, somersaults, on competition equipment.

Format
Small groups by age and ability
Builds
Air sense, core strength, timing, safe landing habits
Setting
Competition trampolines with full safety rigging

Swimming

Format
